How to copy the lorebook:

Works only if the bot creator made the lorebook Public.

  1. Open the desired bot on Janitor.ai
  2. In the more section, find Lorebook and click on it
  3. On the opened page, click on View Script
  4. Click on Clone script
  5. On the new page, click on the icon
  6. To the right of this icon, click on the copy icon
  7. Paste the copied text into the field below and click "Convert".
