📖 How to copy the lorebook:

⚠️ Works only if the bot creator made the lorebook Public.

  1. Open the desired bot on Janitor.ai
  2. In the more section, find Lorebook and click on it
  3. On the opened page, click on View Script
  4. Copy all the content of the opened window (text will start with [, activationMode, etc.)
  5. Paste the copied text into the field below and click "Convert".
